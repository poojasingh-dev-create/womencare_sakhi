# Privacy Policy for SheCare Sakhi

**Last Updated:** November 2026

**SheCare Sakhi** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and safety of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information.

## 1. Information We Collect & How It Is Used
* **Location Data (Optional - Emergency SOS Only):** Location permission is used strictly when you trigger the 3-Tap Emergency SOS feature to generate a Google Maps link of your live location so you can send it to your trusted family contacts via WhatsApp or SMS. We do not track or store your location on any external server.
* **Contacts Permission (Optional):** Used only when you choose to pick an emergency family contact from your phonebook inside the SOS section.
* **Camera & Photo Gallery (Optional):** Used only when you choose to attach photos to your personal recipes or tips. All added photos remain stored locally on your device.
* **Local Storage (Secret Diary, Water, Budget, Period Tracker):** Your Secret Diary PIN, private notes, water intake, budget entries, and period dates are saved locally on your own device using SharedPreferences and are never uploaded to any server.

## 2. Third-Party Services
* **YouTube Player:** Used to play embedded wellness and tutorial videos inside the app.
* **GitHub Content Sync:** Used to fetch updated recipes, yoga tips, and articles.

## 3. Health & Medical Disclaimer
The Period Tracker, BMI Calculator, Yoga, and Beauty tips provided in SheCare Sakhi are for general informational and wellness purposes only and do not constitute professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.

## 4. Children's Privacy
SheCare Sakhi is intended for general audiences and women users and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
